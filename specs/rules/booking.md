# Court Booking Rules

A club that rents out its court(s) turns booking on in its club config:

```json
"booking": {
  "enabled": true,
  "courtLabel": "Pickleball Court",
  "openTime": "08:00",
  "closeTime": "24:00",
  "slotMinutes": 30,
  "minMinutes": 60,
  "creditsPerSlot": 2500,
  "creditsPerDollar": 100,
  "minTopUpDollars": 10,
  "guestHoldMinutes": 15
}
```

`openTime`/`closeTime` are only the defaults: an admin sets the hours on the Settings page ("Court Booking"), saved with the other settings. "24:00" is midnight.

The courts are the club's tables (`tables.all`). Timing Sports has one pickleball court. Anything left out falls back to the values above (shared/rules/booking.js).

## Times

- open 9:00 AM to 12:00 AM midnight for Timing Sports (club time), or whatever an admin has set
- the hours must be on the half hour, with closing at least the minimum booking length after opening (as late as midnight)
- a booking starts on the half hour: 7:00, 7:30, 8:00 ...
- minimum length 1 hour, then in half-hour steps, up to closing or the next booking on that court, whichever comes first
- the last start is 11:00 PM (an hour before closing)
- changing the hours leaves existing bookings alone; the calendar stretches to show any that fall outside them
- a time on an earlier day, or today at or before now, cannot be booked
- two bookings can never overlap: each half hour of a court is claimed once (unique index), so of two people booking the same time at once, exactly one gets it and the other sees "Sorry, that time has just been booked. Please choose another."

## Price

Prices follow the day of the week and the time of day, from `booking.rates` in the club config:

```json
"rates": [
  { "days": ["mon", "tue", "wed", "thu"], "from": "09:00", "to": "18:00", "dollarsPerHour": 30 },
  { "days": ["mon", "tue", "wed", "thu"], "from": "18:00", "to": "24:00", "dollarsPerHour": 40 },
  { "days": ["fri", "sat", "sun"], "from": "09:00", "to": "12:00", "dollarsPerHour": 40 },
  { "days": ["fri", "sat", "sun"], "from": "12:00", "to": "24:00", "dollarsPerHour": 45 }
]
```

- each half hour is charged at the rate covering it, so a booking across 6 PM (or noon) pays each rate for its own part: Mon 5–7 PM is $30 + $40 = $70
- a half hour no rate covers is charged the flat `creditsPerSlot` (the price for a club without rates)
- registered player: the price from their balance — in credits at the top-up rate ($1 = 100 credits: Mon 9–10 AM is 3,000 credits), or for a club on the points model, in dollars ($30). See rules/wallet.md
- guest: the same price in dollars
- the Booking page shows each free half hour's hourly rate; the Pricing page (specs/pages/Club Site.md) is built from the same rates, so the two always agree
- Timing Sports opens at 9:00 AM, where its rates begin

## Booking as a registered player

- signed in with a player account (the admin and tablet logins can only book as a guest)
- the balance is taken atomically: it must cover the cost at the moment of booking, otherwise "Not enough credits. Please top up first." ("Not enough balance. Please top up first." on the points model) and nothing is booked
- every change to a balance is written to a ledger (creditTransactions) with the balance after it

## Booking as a guest

- first name*, last name*, email*, phone* (Canadian/US number)
- the time is held for 15 minutes while the guest pays; a hold that runs out frees the time ("Your hold on this time ran out. Please book again.")
- the booking is confirmed once paid

## Top up

- credits model: $1 = 100 credits, whole dollars, at least $10
- points model: one of the amounts on offer, which adds the same dollars to the balance plus its reward points (rules/wallet.md)
- paid by card

## Payment (Stripe placeholder)

Card payment through Stripe is not connected yet. The payment step shows the amount and a "Card payment (Stripe) — coming soon" area.

- `PAYMENTS_MODE=placeholder` (local development only, never on a Netlify site): a "Simulate payment" button completes the payment without a card
- otherwise: "Online payment is not available yet. Please pay at the club." and the payment cannot be completed
- a payment can be completed once only; a top-up only by the player it is for

## Privacy

Everyone sees which times are booked. The name on a booking is shown only to its owner ("Your booking") and to an admin; everyone else sees "Booked".

## Door code

Once a booking is confirmed (paid with credits, or a guest's card payment), it gets a door code from UniFi Access.

- the code works from 15 minutes before the booking to 15 minutes after it (`doorCodeLeadMinutes`, `doorCodeTrailMinutes` in the club config)
- the booking becomes a UniFi Access visitor (the booker's name, email and phone; remarks "Court booking {ref}") whose visit is that window, with access to the club's doors, and a PIN generated by UniFi is assigned to it. The door only opens to the PIN inside the visit, so nothing needs removing afterwards
- shown to the booker on the confirmation, emailed with the receipt, and shown in My Bookings. Never to anyone else
- if UniFi can't be reached or refuses, the booking still stands (it is paid for): the error is kept on the booking, and the code is tried again whenever the player opens My Bookings, until the window has passed. A visitor left without a PIN is deleted
- two requests at once never make two visitors for one booking
- environment (the Netlify site):
  - `UNIFI_ACCESS_HOST` `https://<console>:12445` — the console must be reachable from the internet (Netlify), not only the club network
  - `UNIFI_ACCESS_TOKEN` — UniFi Access → Settings → General → Advanced → API Token
  - `UNIFI_ACCESS_DOOR_IDS` (comma-separated) and/or `UNIFI_ACCESS_DOOR_GROUP_ID`
  - `UNIFI_ACCESS_ALLOW_SELF_SIGNED=true` — accept the console's own self-signed certificate
- not set up: no code (and no error). `DOOR_ACCESS_MODE=placeholder` (local development only) makes up a 6-digit code instead

## Receipt

Emailed to the booker (the player's email, or the guest's) once the booking is confirmed, after the door code:

- "{club} — Court Booking", "Receipt"
- Booking (ref: the last 8 characters of the booking id), Name, Date, Time, Length, Court
- Paid: "{credits} credits" and "Balance after" (points model: "${amount} from balance" and the balance after in dollars), or "${amount} by card"
- Booked on (club time)
- Liability waiver: "Accepted {date, time} (version {version})", when the club requires one (rules/waiver.md)
- the door code and "Works {from} – {to}", or "Your door code is not ready yet. Please contact the club before your booking."

A failure to send never undoes the booking; it is recorded on the booking (`receiptError`). Sent from the club's Gmail (`GMAIL_1`).
