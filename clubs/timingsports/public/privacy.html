Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Timing Sports ("we", "us") runs an indoor pickleball venue at 140-2668 Simpson Rd, British Columbia, and the website and app at timingsports.ca. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle it in line with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Your account: first and last name, email address, phone number, and — if you give them — sex and date of birth. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash; we never see them.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with us. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Bookings: the court, date and time you book, and for a guest booking the name, email and phone you enter.
  • Payments, balance and rewards: top-ups, what you paid and how, your balance, reward points and the gifts you redeem. Card details are handled by our payment processor and never stored by us.
  • Liability waiver: that you accepted it, which version, and the date and time.
  • Door access: a one-time door code for each booking, valid only around your booking time.
  • On your device: the app keeps you signed in and remembers your language using your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in.
  • To take and manage court bookings, send receipts and door codes, and keep your balance and reward points.
  • To send messages you need: verification codes, receipts and important notices about your bookings.
  • To keep a record of waiver acceptance and payments, as required for safety, accounting and legal reasons.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run parts of the service for us, and only what they need:

  • Netlify (website hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (database).
  • Google (Sign in with Google, and Gmail for sending emails).
  • Twilio (text-message verification codes, if you verify a phone number).
  • Pusher (live updates in the app) and Cloudinary (images).
  • Our door access system (UniFi Access), which receives your name, email and phone with each booking's door code.
  • Our payment processor, when you pay by card.

Some of these providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Booking, payment and waiver records are kept as long as needed for accounting, legal and safety purposes, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and update your details on your Account page at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing us. Some records (such as payments and waiver acceptance) may need to be kept for legal reasons.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can remove our access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to personal information is limited to people who need it to run the club.

Children

Accounts are for adults. A person under 19 may use our services only with a parent or guardian, who accepts the liability waiver on their behalf.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

Timing Sports（“我们”）在不列颠哥伦比亚省 140-2668 Simpson Rd 经营室内匹克球场馆，并运营网站及应用 timingsports.ca。本政策说明我们收集哪些个人信息、用途，以及你的选择。我们依照不列颠哥伦比亚省《个人信息保护法》（PIPA）处理个人信息。

我们收集的信息

  • 账户信息：姓名、邮箱、电话；以及你选择提供的性别和出生日期。密码仅以安全的单向加密形式保存，我们无法看到。
  • 使用 Google 登录：如你选择，Google 会向我们提供你的姓名、邮箱和 Google 账户编号。我们不会获得你的 Google 密码、联系人或其他 Google 数据。
  • 预订：你预订的场地、日期和时间；访客预订时你填写的姓名、邮箱和电话。
  • 付款、余额与积分：充值记录、付款金额与方式、账户余额、积分及兑换的礼品。银行卡信息由支付服务商处理，我们不会保存。
  • 免责声明：你已接受免责声明、所接受的版本及时间。
  • 门禁：每次预订生成的一次性门禁密码，仅在预订时间前后有效。
  • 你的设备：应用使用浏览器本地存储来保持登录状态并记住语言设置。我们不使用广告或追踪类 Cookie。

我们如何使用

  • 创建和管理你的账户，并让你登录。
  • 处理场地预订，发送收据和门禁密码，管理余额和积分。
  • 发送必要信息：验证码、收据及与预订有关的重要通知。
  • 出于安全、会计和法律需要，保存免责声明接受记录和付款记录。

我们不会出售或出租你的个人信息，也不会将其用于第三方广告。

与谁共享

仅与为我们提供服务的服务商共享，且仅限其所需信息：

  • Netlify（网站托管）和 MongoDB Atlas（数据库）。
  • Google（Google 登录，以及用于发送邮件的 Gmail）。
  • Twilio（如你验证手机号，用于发送短信验证码）。
  • Pusher（应用实时更新）和 Cloudinary（图片）。
  • 我们的门禁系统（UniFi Access），每次预订会收到你的姓名、邮箱、电话及门禁密码。
  • 你使用银行卡付款时的支付服务商。

部分服务商可能在加拿大境外（包括美国）存储信息，相关信息可能受当地法律约束。在法律要求时，我们也可能披露信息。

保存期限

账户存续期间我们会保存你的账户信息。预订、付款和免责声明记录会在会计、法律和安全所需期间内保存，之后删除。

你的选择与权利

  • 你可随时在“账户”页面查看和更新个人资料。
  • 你可发邮件要求获取我们所持有的你的个人信息副本、更正信息或删除账户。部分记录（如付款和免责声明接受记录）可能因法律原因需要保留。
  • 如你使用 Google 登录，可随时在 Google 账户设置中撤销我们的访问权限。

安全

信息通过加密连接（HTTPS）传输，密码经过加密处理，只有运营所需的人员才能访问个人信息。

未成年人

账户面向成年人。未满 19 岁者须在父母或监护人同意下使用我们的服务，并由其代为接受免责声明。

政策变更

如本政策有变，我们会更新上方日期；如有重大变更，将通过应用或邮件通知你。
