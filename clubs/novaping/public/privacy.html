Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

NovaPing ("we", "us") is a table tennis club. This policy explains what personal information our website and app collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the privacy laws that apply to us.

What we collect

  • Your account: first and last name, email address, phone number, and — if you give them — sex and date of birth (used for men's, women's and age-group events). Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash; we never see them.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with us. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Club play: the events you register for, your teams and partners, entry fees paid, your match scores and results, and your rating.
  • Liability waiver: if the club asks for one, that you accepted it, which version, and the date and time.
  • On your device: the app keeps you signed in using your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What is public

Like most clubs, we publish results. Your name, rating, match scores and results, and the events and teams you play in, are shown on the site to anyone, including live scores during an event. Your email, phone number, date of birth and payments are never shown publicly.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in.
  • To run events: registrations, groups and draws, schedules, table assignments, scoring, results and ratings.
  • To send messages you need: verification codes and important notices about your account or events.
  • To keep records of entry fees and waiver acceptance.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run parts of the service for us, and only what they need:

  • Netlify (website hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (database).
  • Google (Sign in with Google, and Gmail for sending emails).
  • Twilio (text-message verification codes, if you verify a phone number).
  • Pusher (live score updates) and Cloudinary (images).

Some of these providers may store information outside your country, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Results and ratings form the club's playing history and are kept with it. Payment and waiver records are kept as long as needed for accounting, legal and safety purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and update your details on your Account page at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing us. Results already published may be kept as part of event records, and some records (such as payments) may need to be kept for legal reasons.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can remove our access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to personal information is limited to the people who run the club.

Children

Young players may take part with a parent or guardian, who provides their details and accepts any waiver on their behalf.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
